# Design System: Quiet Engineering

## Direction

The portfolio presents a junior developer through the work itself: a dark,
editorial canvas, restrained UI chrome and one warm material accent. The
signature detail is the Noted. product preview, which replaces simulated IDE
content with a concrete artifact.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#0b1117`
- Surface: `#111820`
- Raised surface: `#18212a`
- Border: `#26313d` / `#52606c`
- Accent: `#b99462` with `#d8bd91` for readable emphasis
- Positive state: `#718a78` / `#9caf9b`
- Primary text: `#f4f1ea`
- Secondary text: `#b8c0c8`
- Muted text: `#8f9aa4`

## Layout principles

- Use left-aligned editorial headings and short readable paragraphs.
- Prefer rules, whitespace and grouped lists over repeated rounded cards.
- Keep technical information in compact category blocks instead of filters,
  search controls or decorative badges.
- Show real work as a product preview; do not simulate an editor or terminal.
- Use a single status marker in the hero and avoid decorative identity badges.

## Sections

1. Hero: direct introduction and an interactive-looking Noted. preview.
2. About: portrait, background and three practical working principles.
3. Projects: one focused Noted. case study with preview, stack and links.
4. Skills: Frontend, Backend & Databases, Tools & QA.
5. Trajectory: BME, Bolyai and real work experience only.
6. Contact: direct channels and message form with simplified location.
